Payments as a module behind a port

“Because we may outgrow the vendor before we outgrow the monolith, and a port costs a week, not a quarter.”

Two revision clouds. One of them will matter in Year 2.

Site inspection, Q3 Y1
Checkout district congested every Friday after 18:00. Not yet a problem. Noted, in pencil.

Complexity +6 · why?

Assumption: a port with a single adapter costs about one engineer-week now and pays back only if the vendor changes. For a team of three that week is 1/18 of the runway to launch.
