# 6. A review board without verdicts

Status: accepted · 2026-10-15

## Context
The course never rewards a single correct answer. It rewards understanding which forces matter, and being able to justify and revisit a decision.

## Decision
No option is marked right or wrong anywhere. Options state what they optimise and what they cost. After each chapter the reader writes a short ADR; the standalone review board asks three questions and, using a deterministic rubric (word counts, mentions of forces and constraints, a revisit trigger that is a condition), says either "answered in your ADR" or "the board would press here". There is no score and no confetti. When a host sensei is available it adds notes alongside, never instead of, the board.

## Consequences
The rubric checks the shape of an argument, not its truth, and can be gamed by keyword stuffing; that is acceptable for self-review and is stated in the UI. The real evaluation, when wanted, comes from the host's sensei.
